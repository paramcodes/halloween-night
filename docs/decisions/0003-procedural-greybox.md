# ADR-0003: Procedural greybox, no downloaded asset dataset

- **Status:** accepted
- **Date:** 2026-10-24
- **Context:** `PLAN.md` §5 describes the Scenario 3D collection: about 39 GB,
  1,342 rows, mostly isolated product shots with no rigs, no modular
  architecture, no LODs, and 4K textures. Downloading it would not produce a
  coherent village, and Phase 3 is the phase that owns art direction.

## Decision

Build Phases 0–2 (and selected Phase 4 depth) as a procedural greybox:

- Every mesh is generated by `MeshFactory` from Godot primitives and merged into
  one `ArrayMesh` per humanoid or prop kind.
- The village is nine houses on a fixed grid plus a gatehouse, a cross street, a
  square, four torches, and props. No `.glb` files are committed.
- A three-hue palette (`Palette`) keeps the greybox readable: cold slate, warm
  amber, dark wood.
- Volumetric fog is omitted. It is a Phase 3/5 art decision and would change the
  perf profile before the baseline is measured.
- Asset directory, provenance ledger, and the Blender/gltf-transform pipeline
  scripts exist, but stay empty until real art is selected.

## Consequences

- The build has no art dependency and reproduces identically on a fresh clone.
- Screenshots prove layout, lighting, and horde readability, not final visual
  quality. `docs/PHASE_STATUS.md` says so explicitly.
- Phase 3 cannot be claimed as done without replacement art and a provenance
  ledger with real rows.
- The dataset decision can be revisited per asset, not as a bulk download. The
  pipeline scripts are the entry point.

## Alternatives rejected

- Bulk-download the dataset and pick through it: 39 GB for unusable product
  shots, plus licence bookkeeping before any art direction exists.
- Buy or generate a character set now: locks the art style before the loop is
  proven, and character animation is explicitly out of dataset scope.
